# Password and Account Policy

## 1. Password requirements

Corporate account passwords must be at least 12 characters long and contain
characters from three of the four following groups: upper-case letters,
lower-case letters, digits, and symbols. The last 10 passwords cannot be
reused. Passwords expire after 90 days; a reminder is sent to the user 14 days,
7 days and 1 day before expiry.

Passphrases of 16 characters or more are accepted with two character groups
instead of three, and are the recommended option for shared workstations at
plant sites.

## 2. What the service desk never does

The service desk never asks a user for their password, over any channel, for
any reason. A message or call requesting a password must be reported to
security@nawa.qa. Temporary passwords are never sent by SMS and never left on
voicemail.

A temporary password is issued to the requester and to nobody else. It is never
given to a line manager, a colleague, an assistant, or any other third party,
even when that person confirmed the requester's identity. A line manager may
confirm an identity; a line manager may not receive the password.

## 3. Password reset

A password reset is a privileged action on the corporate directory. It may only
be performed once the requester's identity has been established.

Identity is established when at least **two independent proofs** are on file,
and at least one of them is either:

- a staff ID checked against the HR record, or
- a confirmation from the requester's line manager.

Security questions alone are never sufficient, whatever their number. Evidence
must be recorded against the ticket at the time it is collected; evidence a
caller asserts but that nothing supports does not count.

When the two-proof rule is not met, the request is escalated to a service desk
supervisor, who either approves the reset with a documented justification or
refuses it. No reset is performed while the escalation is open.

## 4. Temporary passwords

A reset issues a single-use temporary password with a forced change at the next
sign-in. The temporary password expires 4 hours after issue.

The temporary password is communicated to the requester alone, on the channel
recorded in the directory — corporate e-mail or the registered mobile number. It
is never communicated to a third party, and the line manager who confirmed the
request is a third party for this purpose. A request to send a temporary
password to somebody other than the account holder is refused, whoever asks.

If the requester does not sign in within 4 hours, the reset must be requested
again; the expired temporary password cannot be reissued.

## 5. Account lockout

An account locks after 5 consecutive failed sign-in attempts. A locked account
unlocks automatically after 30 minutes. A lockout is **not** a forgotten
password: unlocking an account does not change the password, and resetting a
password does not clear a lockout.

Users who are locked out repeatedly within the same day are checked for a stale
password cached on a mobile device or a mapped network drive, which is the most
frequent cause.

## 6. Shared and service accounts

Shared accounts are only permitted where an application cannot support
individual accounts, and require an exception approved by the IT Security
Office. Their passwords are held in the corporate password vault, rotate every
180 days, and are never reset through the standard service desk procedure.

## 7. Records

Every privileged action on an account is recorded with its author, the evidence
that authorised it, and its outcome. Records are retained for 24 months and are
available to internal audit on request.
