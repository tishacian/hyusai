# Overview

As part of the papAI subscription, a training is delivered to the Client. The course can be tailored to suit specific needs and objectives.

To request a customised training for this course, please contact us to arrange.

This instructor-led, live training is aimed at Data scientists and Data analysts who wish to build, automate, and manage Data Science and Analytics projects using papAI's capabilities.

By the end of this training, participants will be able to:

- Load datasets in papAI to analyse, assess, and quality check data.
- Configure data prep to standardise your datasets.
- Build visual analytics to extract insights.
- Build and train models to identify important variables and meet prediction targets.
- Interpret models to create valuable insights that are useful in making business decisions.
- Monitor and manage models to maintain an optimised prediction performance.

**Format of the Course**

- Interactive lecture and discussion.
- Practice.
- Hands-on implementation in a live-lab environment.
- Course customisation options.

**Duration**

- 7 hours (usually 1 day including breaks)

**Location**

- The training is delivered on your premises.

**Language**

- French
- English

**Requirements**

- Experience with data analytics
- Familiarity with Machine Learning

**Audience**

- Data Analysts
- Data Scientists
