# Service Desk Priorities and Targets

## 1. Channels and hours

The service desk is reachable by phone, by e-mail, at the walk-in desk on the
ground floor of the head office, and through the self-service portal.

Staffed hours are 07:00 to 19:00, Sunday to Thursday. Outside those hours, an
on-call rota covers priority 1 only; everything else is queued for the next
working day.

## 2. Priorities

Priority is set from impact and urgency, by the service desk, not by the
requester. A requester who disagrees escalates to the duty supervisor rather than
re-raising the ticket.

**Priority 1 — a service is down for a site or for a whole function.** Response
15 minutes, resolution target 4 hours. Examples: the plant network at a site,
the payroll application during a payroll run, corporate e-mail for everyone.

**Priority 2 — a service is degraded, or one person is completely blocked.**
Response 1 hour, resolution target 8 working hours. Examples: a suspected
compromised account, a director unable to sign in, a shared drive unavailable to
one team.

**Priority 3 — a service is impaired with a workaround available.** Response 4
working hours, resolution target 3 working days. Examples: a printer at one
floor, a slow application for one user.

**Priority 4 — a request rather than a fault.** Response 1 working day,
resolution target 5 working days. Examples: software installation, an access
request, new equipment.

A password reset with the identity evidence on file is handled at priority 3 and
is expected to complete within the same call.

## 3. What the targets mean

Response is the first substantive human contact, not an automatic
acknowledgement. Resolution is service restored, which may be a workaround; the
underlying problem is then tracked separately.

Targets are measured on working hours for priorities 2 to 4, and on elapsed
clock time for priority 1.

## 4. Escalation

The escalation ladder is: service desk agent, duty supervisor, service desk
manager, head of IT operations. A ticket escalates automatically when it has
consumed 75% of its resolution target.

Any requester may ask for the duty supervisor at any point. A request for
escalation is never refused and is recorded on the ticket.

## 5. Reopening and closure

A ticket is closed once the requester confirms, or after 3 working days without a
reply to a request for confirmation. A closed ticket may be reopened within 10
working days; after that a new ticket is raised and linked to the old one.

Tickets are never closed to meet a target. Closing a ticket that is not resolved
is a disciplinary matter for the agent and is visible in the monthly review.

## 6. Reporting

Volumes, targets met, and reopened rates are reported monthly to the IT
management team, broken down by priority and by category. The report names the
top five categories by volume, which is how candidates for automation are
identified.
