# Joiners, Movers and Leavers

## 1. Principle

Access follows the role recorded in HR. IT provisions what the role entitles,
neither more nor less, and the line manager owns the request. Access granted "for
now, to unblock someone" is the single largest source of audit findings and is
not permitted.

## 2. Joiners

HR notifies IT when the contract is signed, at least 5 working days before the
start date. The notification carries the start date, the role, the site, the line
manager, and the equipment profile.

IT creates the account, the mailbox, and the role-based access, and prepares the
equipment. The account is enabled on the morning of the start date, not before.

The joiner enrols MFA at first sign-in (see Multi-Factor Authentication) and
changes the initial password immediately. The initial password is handed to the
line manager, in person or by phone, and never to the joiner's personal e-mail
address.

Late notification is the usual cause of a joiner without access on day one. When
HR notifies IT with less than 5 working days' notice, the service desk creates
the account within 1 working day but the equipment follows the standard lead
time.

## 3. Movers

A change of role is notified by HR with the effective date. IT removes the
access the previous role entitled and adds the access the new role entitles, on
the effective date.

Access from the previous role is not retained by default. Where a mover needs to
finish a handover, the line manager requests a named extension with an end date,
capped at 30 days.

A change of line manager is notified in the same way: approval routes follow the
manager recorded in HR, so a stale record blocks the mover's own requests.

## 4. Leavers

HR notifies IT of a departure as soon as it is known, with the last working day.

On the last working day: remote access is revoked, then the account is disabled,
then the sessions on all devices are ended. The mailbox is kept for 90 days and
delegated to the line manager on request, after which it is archived for 7
years.

Equipment is returned to the line manager, who confirms the return to IT.
Unreturned equipment is reported to HR after 10 working days.

Accounts are disabled, never deleted: deleting an account destroys the audit
trail attached to it.

## 5. Immediate departures

For a departure effective the same day, HR or the line manager calls the service
desk and confirms in writing within the hour. The service desk revokes access
immediately on the verbal request, because the risk of acting late is greater
than the risk of acting on a call, and records who asked.

## 6. Contractors

Contractor accounts carry an expiry date matching the contract end date, and
expire automatically. A renewal is requested at least 5 working days before
expiry; an expired contractor account is treated as a leaver and is not
reactivated without a new approval.
