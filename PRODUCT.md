# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Agentium serves four layers of users in the same governed workspace:

- Business users who need a direct answer, result, approval, or task without platform vocabulary.
- Business owners who define objectives, understand capabilities, and judge impact.
- Operators and governors who monitor runs, resolve failures, control risk, and audit decisions.
- Technical builders who compose systems, skills, knowledge, models, and flows.

The default experience must match the user's layer. Business work starts with the task. Platform detail remains reachable through progressive disclosure without losing context.

## Product Purpose

Agentium is an operating system for intelligent systems. It turns business objectives into governed executions and measurable outcomes through the loop Objective → System → Run → Outcome → Decision → Control.

Success means users can start useful work immediately, understand what the system is doing, recover when it fails, and reach deeper controls only when their role or task requires them. The product competes on coherence and working value, not feature count.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, and operational. Agentium should feel trustworthy under real work: responsive, legible, technically credible, and free of theatrical AI decoration. Copy is direct and provider-neutral. Status and actions are explicit.

## Anti-references

- A generic AI chatbot or agent playground.
- A feature-catalog SaaS dashboard with many disconnected modules.
- A pipeline-first builder that forces technical concepts on business users.
- Decorative dark-mode AI styling: violet gradients, glass panels, excessive glow, oversized icon tiles, and novelty motion.
- Commercial scaffolding such as pricing, marketplace, billing, certification, or ROI claims when no working product loop supports them.
- Silent stubs, fake runtime bindings, and controls that advertise unavailable behavior.

## Design Principles

1. Start with useful work. Quick Ask and business experiences provide the shortest path from intent to a grounded result.
2. Reveal complexity by role and need. Layer 0 is the business default; operational and technical controls remain available without becoming the default interface.
3. Show real state. Readiness, progress, sources, outcomes, and failures must reflect the runtime rather than optimistic catalog metadata.
4. Make recovery obvious. Errors use safe language and offer the next valid action while preserving user input and any partial result.
5. Prefer coherent loops over feature breadth. A smaller end-to-end workflow that works outranks a visible module with incomplete runtime support.
6. Preserve context. Navigation changes perspective or depth without inventing duplicate hierarchies or breaking the user's workspace and object scope.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for product surfaces. All core actions must work with keyboard and screen readers, expose visible focus, avoid color-only meaning, respect reduced motion, tolerate text scaling and longer translations, and remain usable on narrow touch screens. English and French are first-class and must stay structurally equivalent.
