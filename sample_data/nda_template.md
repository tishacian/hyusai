# Non-Disclosure Agreement Template

## CONFIDENTIALITY AND NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

**Effective Date**: [DATE]

**Between**:
- **Disclosing Party**: [COMPANY NAME] ("Company")
- **Receiving Party**: [VENDOR NAME] ("Vendor")

## 1. Definition of Confidential Information
"Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by either party, including but not limited to:
- Business strategies, plans, and forecasts
- Technical data, designs, and specifications
- Financial information and pricing
- Customer and supplier lists
- Software, algorithms, and proprietary processes
- Any information marked as "Confidential" or "Proprietary"

## 2. Obligations of the Receiving Party
The Receiving Party agrees to:
a) Hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence
b) Not disclose Confidential Information to any third party without prior written consent
c) Use Confidential Information solely for the purpose of the business relationship
d) Limit access to Confidential Information to employees and agents who have a need to know
e) Protect Confidential Information using the same degree of care as its own confidential information, but no less than reasonable care

## 3. Exclusions
Confidential Information does not include information that:
a) Is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Receiving Party
b) Was in the Receiving Party's possession prior to disclosure
c) Is independently developed by the Receiving Party without reference to Confidential Information
d) Is disclosed with the written approval of the Disclosing Party

## 4. Term
This Agreement is effective for a period of [2] years from the Effective Date. Obligations of confidentiality survive termination for an additional [3] years.

## 5. Return of Information
Upon request or termination, the Receiving Party shall promptly return or destroy all Confidential Information and certify such destruction in writing.

## 6. Remedies
The Receiving Party acknowledges that any breach of this Agreement may cause irreparable harm. The Disclosing Party shall be entitled to seek injunctive relief in addition to any other remedies available at law or in equity.

## 7. Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of [JURISDICTION].
