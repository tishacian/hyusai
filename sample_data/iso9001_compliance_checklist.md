# ISO 9001:2015 Compliance Checklist for Vendor Assessment

## Overview
This checklist is used to evaluate vendor compliance with ISO 9001:2015 (Quality Management Systems). It covers the key clauses relevant to procurement qualification.

## Clause 4: Context of the Organization
- [ ] Has the vendor identified external and internal issues relevant to its purpose?
- [ ] Has the vendor determined the needs and expectations of interested parties?
- [ ] Is the scope of the Quality Management System (QMS) clearly defined?
- [ ] Does the QMS documentation include process interactions?

## Clause 5: Leadership
- [ ] Is there evidence of top management commitment to the QMS?
- [ ] Is there a documented Quality Policy?
- [ ] Are organizational roles, responsibilities, and authorities defined?

## Clause 6: Planning
- [ ] Are risks and opportunities identified and addressed?
- [ ] Are quality objectives established and measurable?
- [ ] Is there a plan for achieving quality objectives?
- [ ] Are changes to the QMS planned and controlled?

## Clause 7: Support
- [ ] Are adequate resources provided for the QMS?
- [ ] Is personnel competence determined and maintained?
- [ ] Is there evidence of awareness of the Quality Policy?
- [ ] Are communication processes defined (internal and external)?
- [ ] Is documented information controlled and maintained?

## Clause 8: Operation
- [ ] Are operational planning and control processes documented?
- [ ] Are requirements for products and services determined?
- [ ] Is there a design and development process (if applicable)?
- [ ] Are external providers evaluated and controlled?
- [ ] Is production and service provision controlled?
- [ ] Are nonconforming outputs identified and controlled?

## Clause 9: Performance Evaluation
- [ ] Is monitoring, measurement, analysis, and evaluation performed?
- [ ] Is customer satisfaction measured?
- [ ] Are internal audits conducted at planned intervals?
- [ ] Does top management review the QMS at planned intervals?

## Clause 10: Improvement
- [ ] Are opportunities for improvement determined?
- [ ] Are corrective actions taken for nonconformities?
- [ ] Is continual improvement of the QMS pursued?

## Assessment Verdict
A vendor must meet at least 80% of applicable checklist items to be considered ISO 9001 compliant for procurement purposes.

### Compliance Levels:
- **Fully Compliant**: 90-100% of items met
- **Substantially Compliant**: 80-89% of items met
- **Partially Compliant**: 60-79% of items met (requires remediation plan)
- **Non-Compliant**: Below 60% (disqualification recommended)

## Notes
- This checklist is for assessment purposes only and does not replace formal ISO certification audits.
- Vendors holding a valid ISO 9001:2015 certificate from an accredited certification body automatically meet these requirements.
- Certification expiry dates must be verified. Expired certifications are treated as non-compliant.
