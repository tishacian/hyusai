# Vendor Qualification & Approval Policy

## 1. Purpose
This policy establishes the requirements and procedures for qualifying and approving vendors within the enterprise procurement framework. All vendors must undergo a standardized evaluation process before being authorized to supply goods or services.

## 2. Scope
This policy applies to all procurement activities involving external vendors, suppliers, and service providers across all business units and subsidiaries.

## 3. Required Documents

### 3.1 Mandatory Submissions (Critical)
The following documents must be provided by every vendor and are considered blocking requirements. Failure to submit any of these will result in automatic disqualification.

- **Trade License**: A valid, government-issued trade license corresponding to the vendor's line of business. Must be renewed within the last 12 months. Expired licenses are not accepted under any circumstance.
- **Insurance Certificate**: Comprehensive liability insurance and professional indemnity coverage. Minimum coverage of $2,000,000 USD (or equivalent). Must be valid for at least 6 months from submission date.

### 3.2 Financial Requirements (Major)
These documents are required for qualification but may be submitted with a 30-day remediation window.

- **Financial Statements**: Audited financial statements for the last 2 fiscal years. Must include balance sheet, income statement, and cash flow statement. Unaudited statements require a signed declaration by the CFO.
- **Bank Reference Letter**: A letter from the vendor's primary banking institution confirming the account is in good standing. Must be dated within the last 90 days.

### 3.3 Compliance & Certification (Major)
- **Regulatory Certifications**: Industry-specific certifications relevant to the goods or services provided. ISO 9001 (Quality Management) is recommended for all vendors. ISO 27001 (Information Security) is mandatory for vendors handling data.
- **Environmental Compliance**: Evidence of compliance with applicable environmental regulations. ISO 14001 certification is preferred.

### 3.4 Contractual Documents (Minor)
- **Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)**: A signed NDA using the company's standard template. Must be executed before any proprietary information is shared.
- **Conflict of Interest Declaration**: Signed statement confirming no conflicts of interest with the organization.

## 4. Evaluation Criteria

### 4.1 Severity Classification
Each requirement is classified by severity:
- **Critical**: Missing or expired documents in this category result in immediate disqualification. No exceptions.
- **Major**: Issues require formal remediation within 30 calendar days. Vendor status is set to "Conditional Approval" during this period.
- **Minor**: Advisory findings. Vendor receives a recommendation but is not blocked from proceeding.

### 4.2 Scoring
Vendors are scored on a 100-point scale:
- Documentation completeness: 40 points
- Financial health: 25 points
- Regulatory compliance: 20 points
- References and track record: 15 points

A minimum score of 70 is required for qualification.

## 5. Renewal Requirements
Qualified vendors must undergo re-evaluation annually. All critical documents must be kept current. Lapsed documentation triggers automatic suspension of vendor status.

## 6. Appeals Process
Vendors who are disqualified may submit a formal appeal within 15 business days. Appeals are reviewed by the Procurement Review Board within 10 business days.
