## 1. Severity definitions

| Severity | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| P1 | A covered asset is stopped or unsafe to operate and the customer's production is reduced. |
| P2 | A covered asset is degraded but production is unaffected. |
| P3 | A covered asset shows an early-warning condition with no production effect. |

## 2. Response and restoration commitments

| Severity | On-site response | Full restoration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P1 | Within 4 hours of the incident being opened | Within 24 hours of the incident being opened |
| P2 | Within 12 hours of the incident being opened | Within 72 hours of the incident being opened |
| P3 | Next business day | Within 10 business days |

Both clocks start at the time the incident is opened in the Nordvane service
record, not at the time of the first customer call.

## 3. Service credits

If Nordvane Fluid Systems SA misses the full-restoration commitment for a P1
incident under SLA-PLATINUM-04, the customer is entitled to a service credit of
**EUR 6,500 for each started 12-hour block** beyond the restoration deadline.

Service credits under this policy are capped at EUR 39,000 per incident.

## 4. Temporary reduced-duty operation

A temporary reduced-duty arrangement does **not** stop the restoration clock.
The restoration commitment is met only when the covered asset is returned to its
contracted duty with a permanent repair.

A temporary arrangement is acceptable to Nordvane only when all of the following
are true:

1. The applicable Nordvane safety and maintenance procedure explicitly allows it.
2. It is approved in writing by a Nordvane engineer at the authority level named
   in that procedure.
3. The customer is informed in writing of the duty limit and the time limit.
