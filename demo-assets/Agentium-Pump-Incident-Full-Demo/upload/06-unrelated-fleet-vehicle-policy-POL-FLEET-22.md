# Fleet vehicle policy POL-FLEET-22

**Synthetic demonstration data, deliberately unrelated to rotating equipment.**
This document covers service vans and drivers only. It exists so that a
demonstration can verify that answers stay inside the sources that are actually
relevant. It contains no pump, seal, spare-part or service-level information.

| Field | Value |

| Policy reference | POL-FLEET-22 |
| Policy name | Nordvane service vehicle and driver policy |
| Issued by | Nordvane Fluid Systems SA, Facilities and Fleet |
| Version | 2.1, effective 2026-01-15 |

## 1. Vehicle allocation

Each field-service engineer is allocated one service van. Vans are allocated by
seniority and by the tooling class the engineer is certified for. A van may not
be reallocated between depots without written approval from Facilities.

## 2. Driver requirements

| Requirement | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Licence category | B, valid in the country of assignment |
| Annual driving check | Completed before 31 March each year |
| Maximum continuous driving | 4 hours, followed by a 45-minute break |
| Night driving | Not permitted between 23:00 and 05:00 without dispatcher approval |

## 3. Fuel, tolls and charging

Fuel cards are issued per vehicle, not per driver. Electric vans are charged at
depot chargers whenever the depot is on the route. Toll and parking receipts are
submitted monthly through the expense tool.

## 4. Damage and roadside assistance

All damage is reported to Facilities within 24 hours, whatever the cost. The
roadside assistance contract reference is FLEET-RSA-09.

## 5. Personal use

Limited personal use is permitted outside working hours for engineers on the
standby rota. Personal use is never permitted for vehicles carrying lifting
equipment.
